Ahmed Kamal

Building Hudhud Maps. Previously Uber, Careem, Stream.

I grew up between the Nile and the Mediterranean. I build the data and AI behind products that tens of millions of people across MENA use every day. Right now that means Hudhud, MENA's first local maps.

Before Hudhud, six years at Careem then Uber (2016 to 2022), building the data and ML infrastructure behind the region's super-app: the first big data platform, the ML platform (Yoda) that shipped millions of predictions daily, search ranking, forecasting. Careem was acquired by Uber in 2019 for $3.1B. Then a stretch at Stream, where I founded and sold AI Moderation to B2B customers. Then Riyadh, 2024, to start Hudhud with the founding team.

I write occasionally at blog.ahmedkamal.me.
