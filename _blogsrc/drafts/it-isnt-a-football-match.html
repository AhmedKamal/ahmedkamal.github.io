Today I was having my lunch in a restaurant while the TV was broadcasting the second half of the match between Man City and Sunderland. I always wondered why some people love european football too much. For me it is boring to spend 90 minutes watching a football match unless a very critical one. While I was thinking and waiting for the food of course ☺, Man City scored its first goal. I wasn't concentrating so you can say I missed it. I tried to concentrate a little bit. It was just few minutes until the second amazing goal came. Yea it was really amazing and you can watch it yourself to judge too.

I told myself, OK, the goals are good but it is a one side game like all the ones we see here in Egypt. The surprise happened and Sunderland scored the first goal then the second one! I became excited although I don’t know any info about the match. Lampard entered the field and he didn't waste any time. He scored the third and the decisive goal!

Lampard scored the decisive goal

It isn't a match report!
Why I'm writing about it? This is a real example of the difference between the effect of the genuine, well prepared work and conventional bad managed one. The difference between who keep talking about the new football industry (like in Egypt) and who work to make it real.

I don't have to tell you about football in Egypt because we all know that it has nothing to do with professionalism.

The reasons why the premier league is classified as one of the best football tournaments are very clear for me now. When you make a big and a well managed thing like this, it is not difficult to get more than 21 Billion Egyptian pound as a revenue!

The Premier League has the highest revenue of any football league in the world, with total club revenues of 2.5 billion dollar in 2009–10

A lot of examples including this one tell us that high quality products and professional management are very important for those serious about being the best. Innovation is essential too. Copying doesn’t lead to great success in most cases.

Apple that is worth 479069 $ Million made all this fame and power through delivering products that focused on the needs of users.

I know it was difficult to grasp the previous number so I tried to help!

The need for high quality products, the need for beautiful and exciting products, the need for uniqueness feeling and other needs that apple managed to satisfy and in some cases create too.

It is a new year and if I can say something regarding it, it would be:

Choose something you love. Work on it. Spend too much time working until your results reach the peak state and don’t worry about success!

If you are working on a side project, don't release it just because you have finished it. Analyze the market, test it on few people and get feedback from the real customers.

If it is about learning new thing then read books, watch videos of industry leaders in this field, take notes, teach people the stuff you studied and share the knowledge and you will get a full learning experience.

It is all about doing your homework and yea it isn't easy because if it all people would have done it. No one said that success is easy but it deserves the effort.
