In order for a startup to be really successful at competing with the giants, there are two things that need to be done. The first one is hire the best people you can find in each area and second is to empower them to make the right decisions while solving the problems they meet everyday.

Startups can't afford to operate -like large corporations- as a huge machine that has thousands of gears that fit together to move this giant animal. Instead it rely more on being agile and on empowering people at the front lines to take the right decision. And do you know what does this really require? It requires a high level of ownership, a level that pushes everyone to put the company's future above any personal agenda or career goals.

There are different things that young companies do to reinforce this ownership, the most important one is ensuring there is a strong alignment between the employee's compensation and the company's performance. In the end, there is not much incentive for smart people who know they would be left with nothing if their company did a fantastic exit.
