Hi, few days ago I received a question from one of Egyptian Geeks group members asking me if I can help him with his dream of making a smart robot that could assist humans. I thought this may be a nice thing to share with you so I decided to write my thoughts in a blog so that it can help more people.

Building a smart robot/machine is one of the biggest dreams of all people working in the AI field. A lot of movies have been talking about that dream machine that would be able to learn by itself and act like humans. The question is what should you do if you really want to work on building one of those machines. I can easily extract two challenges from this question.

1- The software challenge
2- The hardware challenge

In this blog post I will concentrate on the SW challenge. I have also asked a friend (Mohamed Showaib) to share with us his thoughts regarding the HW challenge so this would be in a separate blog post isA.

I would first make up some scenarios that we would like our assistant to handle.

1- Simple Question Answering
Questions like:

  • How is the weather like today?
  • Who is the winner of the last world cup?
  • When is my next meeting?
