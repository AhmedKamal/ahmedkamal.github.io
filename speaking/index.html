Speaking

I speak about the systems behind AI products: how they are built, and how the teams around them work. I speak in English and Arabic, to engineering audiences and to executive ones.

I am always happy to talk to people building serious things in the region. It helps to tell me what you would like covered.

What I speak about

  • Machine learning platforms in production. What it takes to serve models reliably, and what breaks when you do it at scale.
  • Automation and human experts as one system. The most durable AI products are not a model. They are a loop: automation proposes, experts correct, the platform learns from the correction. How that loop is designed and measured.
  • How AI is changing engineering practice. The best engineers are writing less code. What that means for how teams are structured, hired, and led.

For organizers

Bio, free to copy into your programme:

Ahmed Kamal builds zero-to-one data and AI platforms. At Hudhud Maps, where he serves as Chief Data & AI Officer, he built the Data & AI team from scratch and took the region's first local mapping platform from proof of concept to public launch in 12 months.

Previously, he co-founded Careem's data platform team to build its first big data infrastructure, then founded its AI team to build Yoda, the machine learning platform shipping millions of predictions daily across 100+ cities through Careem's $3.1B acquisition by Uber. At Stream, he founded and sold the AI Moderation product for infrastructure reaching over a billion end users. He holds special-talent AI residencies in both Saudi Arabia and the UAE.
