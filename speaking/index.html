Speaking

I speak about the systems behind AI products: how they are built, what they cost to run, and how the teams around them work. I lead Data and AI at Hudhud, where we built Saudi Arabia's own map, and I spent six years building the data and ML platforms behind Careem.

I speak in English and Arabic, to engineering audiences and to executive ones. I am always happy to talk to people building serious things in the region.

What I speak about

  • AI, human experts, and platforms as one loop. Keeping a national-scale map current is not a model problem. It is a loop: automation proposes, experts correct, the platform learns from the correction. How that loop is designed and measured.
  • National-scale mapping and computer vision. Building the pipelines behind a country's map, from imagery to road network to points of interest.
  • Machine learning platforms in production. What it takes to serve models reliably, drawn from building Careem's ML platform and shipping predictions at scale.
  • How AI is changing engineering practice. The best engineers are writing less code. What that means for how teams are structured, hired, and led.
  • Building and leading data and AI organizations. Structuring multi-discipline teams across data science, computer vision, engineering, and operations.
